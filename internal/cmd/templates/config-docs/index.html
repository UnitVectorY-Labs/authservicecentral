Understand who can do what

This guide describes how people and workloads receive access to this application. Use it to choose a role, understand the permissions that role grants, and see which resources those permissions can act on.

How access works

Authorization joins identity, a resource-scoped role grant, and an application permission check.

1

Establish identity

A trusted identity source verifies an external token and maps its subject to a platform principal.

2

Grant a role

A role is assigned to that principal or group for a specific audience or application resource.

3

Check permission

The protected application checks a permission against the target resource. Roles are translated into permissions by this policy.
